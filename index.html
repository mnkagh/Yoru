ABOUT

LIMINAL TOKYO is not a travel website. It is a night. You move — the city moves with you. Signs flicker when you look at them. Rain tilts with your cursor. A cat runs if you touch it. Five secrets are hidden in the city. The rooftop is waiting at the end of the night.

Built with Three.js + WebGL. Zero APIs. Every texture, sound and neon sign is generated in your browser. No photos, no cards, no booking forms.
